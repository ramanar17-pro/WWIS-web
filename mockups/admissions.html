The Process

How admissions works

01

Inquiry

Reach out via the contact form or phone. Our admissions team responds with programme fit, availability and next steps.

02

Campus Visit & Assessment

Tour the campus, meet faculty, and complete an age-appropriate readiness assessment for your child's programme (iPlay / iDiscover / iLead).

03

Application Submission

Submit the completed application along with required documents (see checklist below).

04

Family Interview

A short conversation with school leadership to align on your child's needs and WWIS's approach.

05

Offer & Enrollment

Receive your offer, complete enrollment formalities, and get your onboarding pack ahead of the first day.

Questions

Admissions FAQ

Is there an entrance exam?
There's an age-appropriate readiness assessment, not a pass/fail exam — it helps us place your child in the right programme and support level.
Can my child join mid-year?
Yes, subject to seat availability in the relevant programme and grade. Mid-year transfers also require a transfer certificate from the previous school.
What's the minimum age for iPlay?
iPlay is our early-years programme — contact admissions with your child's birth date and we'll confirm exact eligibility for the current intake.
Do you offer sibling priority?
Existing WWIS families receive priority scheduling for tours and assessments — mention this when you submit your inquiry.
